This interactive visualization is built on the Doughnut Economics framework developed by Kate Raworth, measuring social foundations and ecological ceilings. The California data represents state-level performance across 42 indicators as analyzed by the California Doughnut Economics Collaborative (CalDEC).

Visualization powered by the open-source Doughnut Economics Graph project.
